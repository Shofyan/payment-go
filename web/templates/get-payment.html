🔍 Payment Lookup Information

  • Payment IDs are UUIDs generated during payment creation
  • You can find the payment ID in the response after creating a payment
  • Payment status can be: pending, processing, completed, or failed
  • Transaction IDs are assigned by the payment gateway after processing
  • All timestamps are in RFC3339 format

Example Payment ID Format:

123e4567-e89b-12d3-a456-426614174000
